Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our luxury rental requirements, insurance, and delivery.

What documents are required to rent a vehicle? +
You will need a valid driver's license (held for at least 1–2 years), a valid passport or government ID, and a credit card in the primary driver's name for the security deposit. International visitors may present an International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside their home license.
How does the security deposit work? +
We place a temporary pre-authorization hold on your credit card at the time of vehicle handover. Once the vehicle is returned in good order with no traffic violations or tolls pending, the authorization hold is released immediately.
Can I have the vehicle delivered to the airport or my hotel? +
Yes! We provide seamless white-glove doorstep delivery to major airport VIP terminals, private FBO aviation hangars, hotels, and residential addresses.
Is there a daily mileage limit? +
Standard rentals include 150 to 250 miles (250–400 km) per day depending on the vehicle category. Unlimited mileage packages and extended multi-day allowances can easily be added during checkout.
